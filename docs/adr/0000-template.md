# ADR NNNN: Short decision title

- Status: Proposed
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Decision owners: team or role
- Supersedes: none

## Context

Describe the business need, current behavior, constraints, and evidence. State
which LiMon invariants and use cases are affected.

## Decision

State the chosen design precisely, including ownership and layer boundaries.

## Alternatives considered

List realistic alternatives and why they were not selected.

## Consequences

Document benefits, tradeoffs, operational risks, migration needs, compatibility
impact, and future constraints.

## Verification

List tests, metrics, reconciliation checks, or rollout observations that prove
the decision works.

## Rollout and recovery

Describe deployment order, feature flags if any, backfill, rollback, and how to
recover from a partial failure.
